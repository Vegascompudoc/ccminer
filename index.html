What is LoonAI?

LoonAI is the University of Minnesota's umbrella platform for institutionally administered AI tools. Named for Minnesota's iconic common loon, LoonAI brings together a growing suite of AI-powered tools designed specifically for the UMN community — students, faculty, staff, and researchers.

The first project documented in depth under LoonAI is the LoonAI Gateway — a unified access point for AI tools organized across four key use areas: research, teaching, learning, and administration.

All LoonAI tools are administered by the University of Minnesota and operate within UMN's data governance and responsible AI frameworks. For broader university AI strategy and resources, visit the AI Hub at ai.umn.edu.

LoonAI is developed in collaboration with the Office of Information Technology, Data Science & AI Hub, and academic units across the University.
